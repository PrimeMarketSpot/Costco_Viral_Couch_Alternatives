What we are not claiming

An NDA does not create intellectual property rights that do not otherwise exist, and it cannot stop someone who never saw your idea from independently inventing the same thing. No agreement can do either. What this one does is put us under an enforceable duty of confidence before you disclose — which is what makes your disclosure to us a disclosure under confidentiality, capable of supporting rather than defeating the reasonable-measures element of trade secret protection. If your idea is patentable, talk to a qualified attorney about a provisional filing as well. We are not a substitute for counsel, and we will not pretend to be.
