Privacy Notice

Version 1.0.0 — draft pending legal review. Not yet in force.

Read this first. This notice covers personal information — your name, email, billing details, and how you use the site.

It is not what protects your idea. Your idea is protected by the mutual NDA, which is a contract you and we both sign, which we cannot revise unilaterally, and which gives you a cause of action if we break it. We say this here because conflating the two documents is the exact mistake this company exists to correct, and it would be absurd to reproduce it in our own policy.

1. What we collect

1.1 Account and execution data

Your name, email address, and signing capacity; and for each NDA you execute, the agreement version, its hash, the UTC timestamp, your IP address, your user agent, and your recorded assent. This is the evidentiary record of the agreement. It is append-only.

1.2 Submission content

Your ideas and everything you submit under the NDA. This is encrypted under a key unique to your account before it is written to disk. It is governed by the NDA, not by this notice, and the restrictions in NDA §3.2 apply to it in full.

Submission titles are stored unencrypted so your submissions can be listed without decryption. We tell you this at the point of entry and advise putting nothing confidential in a title.

1.3 Billing

Handled by our payment processor. We store a customer reference and subscription state. We never see or store your card details.

1.4 Operational logs

Request metadata — timestamps, endpoints, status codes, IP addresses — retained for 30 days for security and debugging. Log lines never contain submission content.

1.5 What we do not collect

  • No third-party analytics. No Google Analytics, no product analytics SDK.
  • No advertising or tracking pixels, and no cross-site tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies except a strictly necessary session cookie once accounts exist.
  • No data brokers. We buy no information about you from anyone.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts, or stylesheets. Every asset on this site is served by us, so loading a page does not disclose your visit to anyone else.

2. What we do with it

We use personal information only to:

  • provide the services you have asked for;
  • maintain the NDA execution record and make it verifiable;
  • bill you;
  • contact you about your account, security, or changes to these documents;
  • keep the service secure and investigate abuse.

3. What we have barred ourselves from doing

Most privacy policies are lists of permissions. This section is the opposite. We will not, and under the NDA cannot:

  • Train on your submissions. Not our models, not anyone else's; not fine-tuning, distillation, alignment, evaluation, or benchmarking. NDA §3.2(a).
  • Mine your submissions for our own product development, market research, trend reports, or datasets — identified or aggregated. NDA §3.2(b)–(c).
  • Sell, rent, license, or publish your data. To anyone, for any price. NDA §3.2(d).
  • Compete with you using what you told us. We will not build, fund, staff, or advise a venture that competes with one described in your submission. NDA §3.2(e).
  • Read your submissions. Human review is barred except to investigate a specific documented security incident or violation of law, at minimum scope, with notice to you within 72 hours unless a court order prohibits it. NDA §3.2(f).

4. Sharing

We disclose personal information only to:

  • Subprocessors strictly necessary to run the service, each bound by terms at least as protective as the NDA, each listed publicly at /legal/subprocessors.html, with 30 days' notice before any addition. We remain liable for what they do.
  • Legal process, where compelled — and then only the minimum required, with advance notice to you unless we are legally prohibited from giving it, so that you have the opportunity to seek a protective order.

4.1 Acquisition, insolvency, and the clause we did not write

Nearly every privacy policy contains a clause permitting the transfer of your data in a merger, acquisition, asset sale, or bankruptcy. It is so standard that it is rarely read. It is also the clause that decides what happens to your idea on the worst day in the company's life.

Ours is narrower, deliberately:

  • Any successor takes your data subject to the NDA in full, and we must make that an express condition of the transfer. NDA §8.1.
  • You get at least 30 days' notice before any such transfer. NDA §8.2.
  • During that window you may export everything and demand deletion, and we must comply before the transfer completes. NDA §8.2.
  • Your submissions are not a saleable asset of our estate in any insolvency. NDA §8.3.

6. Deletion

On request we delete your submissions and work product within 30 days and issue a signed deletion certificate.

Deletion here is cryptographic, not a flag. Your content is encrypted under a key unique to your account; we destroy that key, which renders any residual ciphertext — in a backup, on a replica, anywhere — permanently unrecoverable. This is the difference between data being marked deleted and data being gone.

The execution record survives, because it proves an agreement existed and both parties may one day need it. It contains no submission content.

7. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you may access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and object to or restrict processing. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days. We do not charge for these requests and we do not require a reason.

Export is available at any time in a non-proprietary machine-readable format, at no charge, including after cancellation. It is never placed behind a payment. NDA §10.

8. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest; per-account content keys; an append-only, hash-chained execution log; least-privilege access. We will notify you of any unauthorised access to your confidential information without undue delay and in any event within 72 hours. NDA §11.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice, and will give 30 days' notice of material changes.

But note the limit of that. This notice cannot expand what we may do with your submissions, because those are governed by an executed contract that we cannot amend unilaterally. A new NDA version applies only prospectively and only if you sign it. Submissions you have already made stay governed by the terms in force when you made them, permanently. NDA §15.2.
