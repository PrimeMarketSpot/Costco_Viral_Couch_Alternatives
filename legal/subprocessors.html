The model provider is the hard one

Everything else on this list handles ciphertext or ordinary account data. The model provider is different: to build your venture, it has to see your idea in the clear at inference time. That makes it the single point where our confidentiality promise could quietly fail.

So it is subject to a hard requirement, not a preference: a contractual zero-data-retention arrangement, under which the provider does not retain, log, or train on what we send. NDA §6.2.

We will not publish a "never trained on" claim before that arrangement is signed. A promise that depends on a vendor term we have not secured is not a promise, and the whole premise of this company is that the difference matters.
