Confidentiality first, disclosure second

The NDA is signed before the idea is typed.

Every other platform asks you to describe your idea, then tells you afterwards what it may do with it. We invert that. A mutual NDA is countersigned, hashed, and independently verifiable before the submission field will accept a single character.

Read and execute the NDA Is your idea protected elsewhere?

You can read the entire agreement before giving us your email, let alone your idea.

Disclosing an idea to a platform under no duty of confidence doesn’t just fail to protect it. It can destroy the trade‑secret status you already had.

Copyright protects expression, not ideas. Patents require a filing. For a raw idea, trade secret law is the only doctrine that covers it at all — and it protects you only while you take reasonable measures to keep it secret.

Typing that idea into a service that owes you no confidentiality is not a reasonable measure. It is the opposite. The submission itself can be the injury, and no amount of careful drafting afterwards undoes it.

The order of operations is the product

Nothing here is unusual technology. What is unusual is doing it in this order, and being willing to be held to it.

  1. You read

    The full agreement, before anything

    No account, no email, no idea. The agreement is served from a single canonical source, hashed in your browser, and checked against the server’s copy so you can be certain the text you read is the text that binds us.

  2. We countersign

    Mutual, and cryptographically signed

    We sign too, with an Ed25519 key whose public half is published. Your countersigned PDF can be verified by your lawyer, or a court, without our cooperation and without our permission.

  3. Then you disclose

    Into a field that was inert until now

    Not just disabled in the page — the API itself refuses submission content from any account with no execution record. There is no code path that receives your idea outside the agreement.

Each promise, and the thing that makes it true

A guarantee you cannot verify is a slogan. Every commitment below is paired with the mechanism that enforces it, because we expect to be checked.

Your idea is never training data

Not for our models, not for anyone’s. Not for fine-tuning, evaluation, benchmarking, or “product improvement”.

Mechanism: a material term of the NDA, not a policy we can revise — plus zero-data-retention endpoints required of any model vendor, flowed down contractually.

You own everything

The idea, the code, the copy, the designs, the brand, the data. Assignment runs to you, at the moment of creation.

Mechanism: work-made-for-hire with a present-assignment fallback, plus a perpetual licence to any platform component embedded in your work so you can leave with it intact.

0% of your revenue

We are never in your payment path. Your Stripe account, your money, your withdrawal schedule. No holds, no caps, no minimums.

Mechanism: we hold no connected account on your behalf and take no security interest. There is no plumbing through which a cut could be taken.

An acquisition can’t launder the duty

If we are bought, or fail, the obligation travels with the data. Your idea is not an asset in our estate.

Mechanism: the NDA binds successors and assigns, with 30 days’ notice, an export window, and a right to demand deletion before any transfer completes.

Non-payment never holds your work hostage

Cancel, lapse, or walk away: export stays open and stays free. Suspension stops our performance, never your access.

Mechanism: a contractual 90-day post-termination export guarantee, and an express bar on putting export behind a payment.

Deletion that actually deletes

Ask, and it is gone in 30 days, with a signed certificate confirming it.

Mechanism: your content is encrypted under a key unique to your account. Deletion destroys the key, which renders any residual ciphertext cryptographically unrecoverable.

What we will not claim

The industry we are competing with over-promises about idea protection, so it would be a poor start to do the same.

  • An NDA does not create IP rights that don’t otherwise exist.
  • An NDA cannot stop independent invention by someone who never saw your idea. Nothing can.
  • An NDA is not a patent. If your idea is patentable, speak to an attorney about a provisional filing — do that as well as this, not instead of it.
  • We are not your lawyer, and nothing on this site is legal advice.

What an NDA does is place us under an enforceable duty before you speak, and give you a cause of action if we break it. That duty is precisely what is missing today — and it is enough to keep your disclosure consistent with trade secret protection rather than destructive of it.
