Why there's no password

We ask you to keep as little with us as possible, so it would be strange to insist on holding a credential we don't need. Email possession is already the recovery factor behind every password system — a password just adds something for us to leak, and something you have probably reused somewhere that matters more than here.

Sign-in links are single use. Following one consumes it, so a link sitting in an old inbox, a proxy log, or your browser history isn't a way in.
