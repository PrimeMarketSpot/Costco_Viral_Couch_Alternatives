Verify a signature

Confirm that a countersigned agreement is exactly the document that was signed, and that our countersignature is authentic.

This page is public and requires no account. A confidentiality guarantee that only the guarantor can audit is not a guarantee — so the audit is open to anyone holding a copy: you, your lawyer, a court, or an adversary.

Printed on the cover sheet of every countersigned PDF.

If you paste the text from your PDF, we will hash it and tell you whether it is byte-for-byte what was executed. Leave blank to just look up the record.

Verifying without us

You do not have to trust this page. Everything it does can be reproduced offline:

  1. Take the agreement text from your countersigned PDF.
  2. Compute its SHA-256 digest — shasum -a 256 agreement.txt.
  3. Compare it to the document hash printed on the cover sheet.
  4. Verify our Ed25519 countersignature against the canonical execution record using our public key, published at /api/verify-nda?publicKey=1.

The signing key is asymmetric on purpose. A shared-secret signature would let us prove a countersignature only to ourselves, which is worth nothing to you in a dispute. With a published public key, our signature can be checked by someone who does not trust us and whom we cannot stop.

Log integrity

Execution records are append-only and hash-chained: each carries the digest of its predecessor, so a record cannot be removed, reordered, or quietly edited without breaking every link after it. You can check the chain at /api/verify-nda?audit=1.
