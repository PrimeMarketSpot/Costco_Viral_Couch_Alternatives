What we are not saying

We are not saying the competitor has done anything unlawful, or that it has misused anyone’s idea. We have no evidence of either and we make no such claim. Every row above describes what the operative documents permit — which is the only thing a prospective user can actually rely on when deciding whether to disclose.

Permissions matter regardless of current conduct, because they are what survives a change of management, a change of strategy, or an acquisition. A company that behaves well today while reserving the right to train on your idea and transfer it in an asset sale has reserved those rights for a reason.

We have also linked their pages directly rather than only quoting them. Read the originals. If we have got something wrong, tell us and we will fix it in public.
